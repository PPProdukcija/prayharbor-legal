Privacy Policy

PrayHarbor Privacy Policy Effective: October 4, 2026. PrayHarbor stores your optional name, prayer preferences, prayer history and guard settings on your device. App selection is managed through Apple Screen Time APIs using opaque selection tokens. We do not operate a user account service or collect these records on our server in this version. Apple processes purchases and may process diagnostics according to your device settings. No advertising tracking is included. You may clear prayer history in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data subject to Apple device backups. You can revoke Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings. Purchase cancellation is managed through your Apple Account and is separate from deleting the app. Remote AI personalization is not enabled in this version. If added, we will explain what is sent and update this policy before use. Contact Pavle Pejic: ppejic89@gmail.com.
